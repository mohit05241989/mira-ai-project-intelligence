# Q3 – Program Charter
# ABCDE Ltd. – AI Adoption Project

## Purpose
Support ABCDE Ltd.'s adoption of AI-powered capabilities across selected logistics and customer-support functions.

## Business Need
The initiative evaluates practical AI use cases to improve operational intelligence and customer experience.

## Objectives
- Assess current processes and systems.
- Identify data sources and gaps.
- Prioritize AI use cases.
- Design and implement pilots.
- Evaluate pilot outcomes.
- Progress selected solutions toward broader deployment.
- Establish monitoring and review.

## Scope
Current-state analysis, data assessment, AI use-case selection, pilot design, model development/testing, pilot implementation, evaluation, iteration, deployment, training and monitoring.

## AI Use-Case Areas
- Demand forecasting
- Route optimization
- Inventory management
- Customer support

## Major Deliverables
- Project charter
- Team structure
- Current systems audit
- Gap analysis
- Prioritized AI use-case list
- Prototype models
- Pilot results
- Updated models/process refinements
- Production rollout
- Training completion
- Project closure report

## Stakeholders
Business sponsors, PM/TPM team, technical/AI team, data stakeholders, operations/logistics, legal/compliance, end users and vendors.

## Governance
Regular reviews should cover schedule, risks/issues, deliverables, decisions, dependencies, stakeholder concerns and AI/model performance where applicable.

## Timeline
Six-month / 24-week initiative:
1. Project Initiation – Weeks 1–2
2. Current State Analysis – Weeks 3–4
3. Use Case Selection – Weeks 5–6
4. Pilot Design – Weeks 7–10
5. Pilot Implementation – Weeks 11–14
6. Evaluation & Iteration – Weeks 15–16
7. Full Deployment – Weeks 17–22
8. Monitoring & Review – Weeks 23–24

## Key Risk Categories
Data quality/availability, integration, AI/ML skills, change adoption, ethical/legal concerns, cost, expectations, model performance, security and vendor dependency.

## Success Criteria
Completion of agreed deliverables, successful evaluation of pilots, appropriate stakeholder adoption, satisfactory model performance, effective risk management and readiness for approved production deployment.
